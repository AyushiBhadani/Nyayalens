EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This Employment Contract ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of March 15, 2024, between:

EMPLOYER: TechNova Solutions Inc., 500 Innovation Drive, Austin, TX 78701
EMPLOYEE: Sarah Johnson, 22 Sunrise Blvd, Austin, TX 78702

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee is hired as a Senior Software Engineer. Employee agrees to perform all duties as assigned by the employer, which may be changed at any time without notice.

2. COMPENSATION
Base Salary: $95,000 per year, paid bi-weekly.
Bonuses: At the sole discretion of management. No guaranteed bonuses of any kind.

3. WORKING HOURS
The standard work week is 40 hours. However, employee agrees that additional hours may be required without additional compensation when deemed necessary by management.

4. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT
This is an at-will employment agreement. Either party may terminate this agreement at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice.

5. NON-COMPETE CLAUSE
For a period of TWO (2) YEARS following termination of employment for any reason, Employee agrees not to:
- Work for any competitor within the United States.
- Start any business that competes with TechNova Solutions.
- Solicit any current clients or employees of TechNova Solutions.
Violation of this clause will result in liquidated damages of $50,000.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
ANY work created by the Employee, whether during work hours or personal time, whether using company equipment or personal equipment, shall be the exclusive property of TechNova Solutions Inc.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee agrees to maintain strict confidentiality of all company information indefinitely, even after termination of employment.

8. BENEFITS
Health Insurance: Employer covers 50% of premiums.
Paid Time Off: 10 days per year for the first 3 years.
Benefits may be modified or terminated at employer's sole discretion at any time.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration. Employee waives the right to a jury trial and the right to participate in any class-action lawsuit.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
