RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2024, between:

LANDLORD: Greenfield Properties LLC, 123 Oak Street, Springfield, IL 62701
TENANT: John Doe, 456 Maple Avenue, Springfield, IL 62702

PROPERTY: The landlord agrees to lease to the tenant the premises located at 789 Elm Drive, Apartment 4B, Springfield, IL 62703 ("Premises").

1. TERM
The lease term shall commence on February 1, 2024, and shall continue for a period of twelve (12) months, ending on January 31, 2025.

2. RENT
Tenant agrees to pay $1,500 per month, due on the 1st of each month. A late fee of $150 shall be charged for any payment received after the 5th of the month. Rent shall be paid by bank transfer only; cash payments will not be accepted.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,000 (two months' rent) upon signing. The landlord may deduct any amount from the deposit for ANY damages, cleaning fees, or unpaid rent at sole landlord discretion, without providing an itemized list to the tenant.

4. AUTO-RENEWAL CLAUSE
This lease shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of termination at least 90 days prior to the end of the current term. Failure to provide timely notice will bind the tenant to another full year of rent obligations.

5. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE
Tenant is responsible for all utilities. Additionally, tenant is responsible for ALL repairs and maintenance costs up to $500 per incident, regardless of the cause of damage, including normal wear and tear.

6. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
Landlord reserves the right to enter the premises at any time without prior notice for inspection, repairs, or any other purpose deemed necessary by the landlord.

7. SUBLETTING
Tenant may not sublet the premises under any circumstances. Violation of this clause will result in immediate termination and forfeiture of the entire security deposit.

8. PETS
No pets of any kind are permitted. Violation results in a $1,000 fine per incident and may result in immediate eviction.

9. EARLY TERMINATION
If the tenant wishes to terminate the lease early, they must pay the equivalent of THREE months' rent as a penalty, in addition to forfeiting the security deposit.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Landlord shall not be liable for any injury, damage, or loss to tenant or tenant's property, including injuries resulting from landlord's own negligence.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
